Current finding

Voynich suffix ordering remains modestly self-clustering under page-level resampling. Prefix ordering is near neutral. After boundary-safe comparator tokenization, all tested comparator full-corpus minimum-side point estimates are below 1.0, and all 14 large-comparator minimum-side intervals are below 1.0 in the declared 28,447-token deletion-stability analysis.

At the 14,380-token all-system target, Arabic and Georgian minimum intervals cross neutral. That weaker sensitivity is part of the result, not a footnote to be misplaced in a drawer.

Scope

This finite-corpus result does not establish natural-language uniqueness, identify a language, decipher the manuscript, infer syntax or semantics, or identify a generating mechanism.

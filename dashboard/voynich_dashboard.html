What Version 3 says

  • For a class occurring k times in a unit of length n, the exact random-order expectation for adjacent self-transitions is k(k-1)/n.
  • In the 28,447-token matched analysis, Voynich prefix ordering is near neutral and suffix ordering is modestly self-clustering. All 14 tested large comparators have their entire minimum-side replicate interval below neutral.
  • At the smaller 14,380-token target including Ottoman Turkish, the Voynich minimum-side interval crosses 1.0. That sample-size sensitivity is part of the result.
  • This is a finite-corpus structural contrast. It is not a language classifier.

What it does not establish

Version 3 does not decipher or translate the manuscript, identify a language, establish natural-language uniqueness, infer syntax or semantics, or identify a cipher, constructed-language, stenographic, hybrid, or other generating mechanism.

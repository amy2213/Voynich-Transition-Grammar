Bounded conclusions

  • Under the canonical prefix-first EVA classifier and strictly within lines, CHEDY to QOK is elevated and AIIN to QOK is suppressed relative to the declared independence baseline.
  • Under identical automatic affix discovery, preserved natural boundaries, exact size matching, and repeated block-aware sampling, Voynich has a higher minimum-side self-clustering estimate than each of the 14 eligible frozen comparators.
  • These are corpus-structure findings. They do not decipher or translate the manuscript, identify a language, prove natural language, or exclude untested constructed, hybrid, stenographic, cipher, or historical mechanisms.
